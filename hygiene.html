Track A: Cosmetic Scratches & Teething Marks ($0 Fee)

Light play wear is naturally expected! Our craftspeople sand, re-stain with mineral dyes, and buff with organic beeswax to make the toy look brand new.

Track B: Missing Puzzle Piece or Wooden Gear ($0 Fee)

Health & Safety Guidelines

At-Home Play & Sickness Protocols

Simple, pediatrician-approved rules to keep your toy rotations pristine and safe while in your home.

1. Daily Wipe-Down: Water Only

Wipe wooden surfaces with a barely damp microfiber cloth and let air dry. Never submerge wooden toys in water or use bleach sprays, which strip natural beeswax.

2. Sickness Isolation Bag Included

If your little one catches flu, RSV, or strep, place toys in the designated quarantine bag. When returned, they undergo our double-cycle medical UV-C sterilization at no extra charge.

3. Natural Sunlit Storage

Store toys in dry, ventilated playroom spaces. Natural sunlight is a gentle organic sanitizer, while high-humidity bathrooms should be avoided.

4. Need an Early Swap After Sickness?

Request an instant sick-bay swap through your Parent Portal dashboard. We will dispatch a freshly sterilized replacement box to your door right away.
