Hygiene & Safety

How do you sanitize toys between home rentals?

Every toy undergoes our certified 5-stage cleaning protocol: organic botanical ultrasonic scrub, medical-grade 254nm UV-C germicidal chamber exposure, high-temperature steam sterilization, food-grade eco-wax buffing, and vacuum-sealed packaging before dispatch.

Rotations & Delivery

What happens if a toy component or piece goes missing?

Kids play hard and curiosity is encouraged! Normal wear and tear is covered 100% by our PlayLoop Peace-of-Mind guarantee. If a small component is misplaced, we send a replacement part or provide a 1-week grace search window at no penalty.
