If your child opens a box and doesn't connect with a toy within the first 7 days, trigger an instant replacement in your member dashboard. We dispatch an alternative curated collection immediately with zero penalty fees.

2

Mid-Cycle Accelerated Rotation (14 Days)

Standard, Premium, and Hybrid plan members unlock free early rotation eligibility after just 14 days of receipt. Perfect for fast learners and developmental milestones leaps.
